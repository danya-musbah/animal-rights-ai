# Major Animal Welfare and Animal Rights Organizations — Overview

**Document type:** Organization report / educational material (original text)
**Jurisdiction:** International

> This is an original, independently written overview naming several well-known
> organizations. It describes their general focus and orientation at a high level only,
> and does not reproduce their internal materials, campaign statistics, or exact
> statements. For current programs, campaigns, or positions, consult each organization's
> official website directly.

## Welfare-Oriented Organizations

**RSPCA (Royal Society for the Prevention of Cruelty to Animals)** — Founded in England in
1824 (making it one of the oldest animal welfare organizations in the world), the RSPCA
works on animal cruelty investigation and prosecution support, rescue and rehoming, and
welfare-standard advocacy in England and Wales. Sister/independent organizations exist in
other jurisdictions (e.g. Scotland's SSPCA, Australia's RSPCA Australia).

**The Humane Society of the United States (HSUS) / Humane World for Animals** — A major US
animal protection organization working across companion animal welfare, farm animal
welfare policy, wildlife protection, and disaster response, and engaging in legislative
advocacy at state and federal level in the US.

**World Organisation for Animal Health (WOAH)** — see
`international/woah_animal_welfare_standards_overview.md` for its standard-setting role;
technically an intergovernmental body rather than an NGO, but central to international
animal welfare standard-setting.

**Compassion in World Farming (CIWF)** — A UK-founded international organization focused
specifically on farm animal welfare policy and legislative reform, including on issues such
as caged farming and live animal transport.

## Rights-Oriented / Abolitionist Organizations

**PETA (People for the Ethical Treatment of Animals)** — An international organization
that takes an explicit animal rights position (in the sense described in
`animal_welfare/animal_rights_vs_animal_welfare.md`), campaigning against the use of
animals in food, clothing, entertainment, and research, often through public
awareness campaigns.

**Various regional and national abolitionist groups** exist across many countries,
generally arguing for an end to animal use as property rather than only regulating that
use; specific claims about any individual organization's current campaigns or positions
should be verified against that organization's own published materials.

## Conservation / Wildlife-Focused Organizations

**IUCN (International Union for Conservation of Nature)** — maintains the widely cited "Red
List" of threatened species, informing conservation policy and CITES appendix decisions
(see `international/cites_convention_summary.md`).

**WWF (World Wide Fund for Nature / World Wildlife Fund)** — an international conservation
organization working on habitat protection, species conservation, and (increasingly)
sustainable use and anti-trafficking policy.

## A Note on Neutrality

This knowledge base does not endorse any single organization's ethical position, campaign,
or fundraising claims. When a user's question concerns a specific organization's stance,
the assistant should describe that organization's *publicly stated* position as accurately
as the retrieved evidence allows, and distinguish it clearly from either established law or
